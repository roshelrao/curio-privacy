Managing or canceling your subscription

Curily Pro subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. To manage, change, or cancel your subscription:

  • Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  • Tap your name at the top, then tap "Subscriptions"
  • Select Curily and make your changes

Canceling stops future renewals — you'll keep Pro access until the end of the current billing period.

Restoring a purchase

If you've reinstalled the app or switched devices, open Curily's paywall screen and tap "Restore Purchases" to reactivate Pro using the Apple ID that originally purchased it.

Syncing your data across devices

Sign in with Apple or email in Settings to back up your Brain Dates, streaks, and progress, and access them on another device.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account and all synced data from Settings → "Delete Account & Data."
